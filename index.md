# von

**von** is a private, single-user personal assistant application. It runs on a
server operated solely by its owner and is not offered to, distributed to, or
usable by the general public.

## What it does

von helps its owner organise personal information — reading email and calendar
entries in order to summarise, search, and answer questions about them.

## Access requested

von requests read-only access to Google account data:

- `gmail.readonly` — view email messages and settings
- `calendar.readonly` — view calendars and events

von cannot send, modify, or delete email. It cannot create or change calendar
events. It has no access to Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Contacts.
